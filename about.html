About Glamour Touch

Discover who we are, what we believe in, and why our clients choose Glamour Touch Salon & Makeup Studio.

Our Story

Glamour Touch Salon & Makeup Studio is a modern beauty salon established in 2023 in Sandton, Johannesburg, Gauteng.

The salon was created to provide professional and affordable beauty services in a relaxing and welcoming environment. Glamour Touch specialises in hair styling, professional makeup, nail care, eyelash extensions, eyebrow shaping, and skincare treatments.

Our goal is to help every client look and feel their best through quality beauty services, personalised experiences, and excellent customer service.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality beauty services that enhance confidence, promote self-care, and ensure every client enjoys a professional and relaxing salon experience.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading beauty salons in Gauteng by offering exceptional beauty services, embracing new beauty trends, and building long-lasting relationships with clients.

Our Values

  • Quality: We are committed to providing high-quality beauty services and products.
  • Professionalism: We maintain professional standards in everything we do.
  • Customer Care: Every client deserves to feel valued, respected, and comfortable.
  • Confidence: We aim to help our clients feel confident and comfortable in their own style.
  • Creativity: We embrace creativity and current beauty trends to create personalised looks.
